What Changed This Quarter

  • −Growth stalled. Revenue is up just 0.8% YoY and down 7.7% QoQ — the first sequential revenue decline across the last five quarters, breaking a run the company had called "seven consecutive quarters of growth" as of Q3 FY26.
  • −Profit fell YoY for the first time in this stretch. Net profit of ₹31.0 Cr is below both the year-ago quarter (₹31.6 Cr) and the prior quarter (₹37.0 Cr), pulling PAT margin down to 19.6% — its lowest since Q2 FY26.
  • −Order book is shrinking. Down to ₹876 Cr as of Mar-26, from a ₹1,092 Cr peak in Sep-25 — a ~20% decline that sits uneasily next to management's stated aim to match or exceed FY26 order inflow through FY27.
  • •Base effect is real. Q1 FY26 itself was an outsized quarter (+112% YoY revenue, +166% YoY profit), so some of this quarter's flat YoY optics reflect a hard comp, not necessarily a broken business.
  • •Valuation has already de-rated. Shares sit near their 52-week low at ~13.6× TTM earnings, down 17% over one year — the market was pricing in some of this slowdown ahead of today's print.

Quick Take

A momentum pause, not yet a breakdown. Q1 FY27 snapped a five-quarter run of sequential revenue growth and delivered the first YoY profit decline in this window, with the order book trending the wrong way for two straight halves.

The counterweight is a tough base quarter and a stock that's already de-rated to the high-teens. Whether this is order-timing noise or a real deceleration hinges on what management says next — not on this print alone.
