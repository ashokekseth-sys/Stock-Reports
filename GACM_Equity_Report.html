Company Overview & Business Model

What They Do

GACM Technologies Limited was incorporated in 1995 and, until 2022-23, operated as Stampede Capital Limited — a stockbroking and Portfolio Management Services (PMS) firm. The company discontinued its stock-broking business in 2022, surrendered its PMS licence, and was renamed GACM Technologies Limited via a fresh Certificate of Incorporation dated 4 May 2023. It now describes itself as being engaged in financial, management, business and other ancillary consultancy services, alongside financial-technology (fintech) related services.

This is a fundamental change of business rather than an organic evolution of the old broking franchise, so the pre-2023 track record under "Stampede Capital" has limited read-through to the current consultancy/fintech model. The company has since undertaken a series of large, unusual corporate actions relative to its size — a rights issue (Jan 2025, undersubscribed), a ₹200 Cr QIP private-placement announcement (Jul 2025), a proposed UK subsidiary and share-swap plan, and a board-approved US$699 million FCCB issuance (Apr 2026) — discussed in Section 05.

There is also a separate DVR (Differential Voting Rights) share class (NSE: GATECHDVR) linked to the same group, which is not covered in this report — figures here relate to the ordinary equity shares (BSE: 531723).

This is the single biggest governance flag in this report. Promoter holding of under 1% is exceptionally low for a listed Indian company — the founders/promoters have almost no direct economic stake left in the business relative to public shareholders. Publicly available data lists one non-promoter individual investor, Venkata Jonna, holding ~5.07% of the company — a larger stake than the promoter group itself. Near-zero promoter holding, combined with a history of large equity issuances (rights issue, QIP, and a proposed FCCB), is a pattern that typically raises questions about who ultimately controls the float and why capital keeps being raised at a company of this size. Investors should independently verify the current shareholding pattern on the BSE/NSE corporate filings before relying on this figure, since it changes every quarter and this company issues shares unusually often.

Investment View & Recommendation

★ Recommendation
HIGH RISK / SPECULATIVE — NOT SUITABLE FOR CORE INVESTMENT

GACM Technologies shows real revenue and profit growth off a very small base and screens statistically "cheap" on P/E and P/B. But the combination of (a) 0.91% promoter holding, (b) a SEBI order on record under its former name, (c) a pattern of large, repeated capital raises culminating in a US$699 Mn FCCB proposal roughly 90x its market cap, and (d) sub-₹1 price with no analyst coverage, together constitute governance and disclosure risk that outweighs the headline valuation and growth numbers for most investors. This profile fits a small, high-risk speculative trading position at most — not a core portfolio holding — and only for investors who fully understand and accept the possibility of permanent capital loss.

Before Considering Any Position
  • Read the full SEBI order (Jan 2025) on sebi.gov.in relating to GACM Technologies / Stampede Capital.
  • Verify the latest quarterly shareholding pattern directly on BSE/NSE, since promoter/public holding here has been unusually fluid.
  • Track FCCB approval status (SEBI/RBI/MCA/FEMA) rather than assuming the in-principle BSE nod means the funds are secured.
  • Size any position as strictly speculative capital, with a firm stop-loss, given the stock's low price, low liquidity and volatility.
