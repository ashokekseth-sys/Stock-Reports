Company Overview

What They Do

Vedanta Aluminium Metal Limited (VAML) is India's largest aluminium producer, carved out of Vedanta Ltd via a 4-way demerger (NCLT-approved Dec 2025, effective 1 May 2026) and independently listed on 15 June 2026. The business runs the full value chain — bauxite mining, the 5 MTPA Lanjigarh alumina refinery (Odisha), and ~2.88 MTPA of combined smelting capacity across Jharsuguda (one of the world's largest single-location smelters) and BALCO (Korba, Chhattisgarh) — giving it a dominant 55-60% share of India's primary aluminium production and customers in 60+ countries.

Q1 FY27 — its first quarter as a standalone listed entity — delivered record aluminium output of 632 KT, revenue of ₹21,105 Cr (+45% YoY), and PAT of ₹6,597 Cr (+205% YoY) at a record 50% EBITDA margin. Cost of production is guided at $1,450-1,500/t, underpinned by captive alumina, captive power and captive coal — the core of the company's cost-curve advantage.

Key Competitive Advantage: Full upstream integration — captive bauxite, captive alumina (Lanjigarh), and captive coal-based power — pushing cash costs toward the first quartile of the global cost curve ($1,450-1,700/t range guided). This is a cost moat, not a brand or IP moat: aluminium ingot is fundamentally an LME-priced commodity, so Vedanta competes on being the lowest-cost producer, not on differentiation.

What Happened & Why It Matters Beyond the Headline

Vedanta Ltd's four-way demerger (aluminium, oil & gas, power, iron & steel, with a residual Vedanta Ltd) became effective 1 May 2026, and the four new entities began independent trading on 15 June 2026. VAML — the aluminium business — was immediately the group's largest listed entity by market cap, debuting at ₹522-527/share (~₹2.06 lakh Cr).

The near-term catalyst that matters most: analysts flag VAML's likely entry into the Nifty Next 50 at the September 2026 rebalancing, at an estimated weight of ~3.4%, which would trigger an estimated ₹1,300 Cr of passive index-fund inflows — a mechanical, dateable demand event distinct from the fundamental story. This is the single most specific technical catalyst in this report.

Caveat: despite record Q1 FY27 results (PAT +205% YoY), the stock is down 4.7% since listing — a genuine disconnect between fundamentals and price action that likely reflects a mix of post-demerger technical selling (shareholders receiving shares "for free" and trimming), T2T restriction in the first 10 sessions limiting liquidity, and MarketSmith's own POOR price-strength reading.
Framing note: This is a newly-listed entity with almost no independent trading history (~2 months, 41 sessions) — every technical read in this report (52-week range, relative performance, RSI) should be understood as "since listing," not a mature multi-year pattern. The Nifty Next 50 inclusion decision (expected Sep 2026) is the next concrete date to watch.

He succeeds Rajiv Kumar, who had been named aluminium-business CEO in March 2025 ahead of the demerger. This is a very recent, still-unsettled leadership transition worth monitoring for continuity of strategy.
Promoter Structure & Encumbrance
100% of Stake Encumbered
Promoter group (Vedanta Resources subsidiaries — Twin Star Holdings, Welter Trading, Vedanta Holdings Mauritius II) holds 56.38%, and per the July 2026 GLAS Agency disclosure, the entire holding is encumbered against a $1.75bn bond facility. A parallel 56.38% encumbrance also exists on Vedanta Iron & Steel promoter shares for a separate $2.25bn facility — the group-wide pattern is consistent and material.
Governance & Auditor
Auditor Changed Pre-Listing
Statutory auditor S R B C & Co. LLP (Ernst & Young network) was appointed to fill a casual vacancy after the prior auditor resigned around 12 Jun 2026 — just days before the 15 Jun listing. An auditor change this close to a listing warrants scrutiny, though no specific irregularity has been publicly identified. SEBI has separately been investigating Vedanta Resources/group entities (per Viceroy Research reporting, Oct 2025) and issued a compliance warning that contributed to demerger delays (Aug 2025).
Capital Allocation
First Interim Dividend ₹8/sh
Declared alongside Q1 FY27 results (>₹3,000 Cr aggregate payout) — continuing the Vedanta group's tradition of aggressive dividend distribution, historically linked to servicing parent Vedanta Resources' debt. Management states growth capex (₹13,226 Cr committed) and deleveraging are not mutually exclusive given current free cash flow strength.

Debt / Financial RisksVAML-level metrics are genuinely improving (net debt/EBITDA 0.9x, down from 1.3x; ICRA AA+/Stable). The sentence that would make an investor pause: VAML delivered a genuinely record fundamental quarter — 205% PAT growth, 50% EBITDA margin, a decade-best credit rating — and the stock is still down since listing, with its entire promoter stake encumbered against a parent-level debt facility and both its CEO and auditor having changed within weeks of the IPO; the operating story is excellent, but the governance and technical picture argue for patience over urgency.
